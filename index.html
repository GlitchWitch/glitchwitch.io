RIP GlitchWitch Security

Since 2017, Jade has gained global recgonition in the infosec community under the hacker handle GlitchWitch. As an information security researcher–turned–infosec company founder, they've contributed to a range of vulnerability disclosure programs (most undisclosed), left an imprint at the occasional conference, and across various internet subcommunities along the way.

In 2022 they switched gears, moving away from their independent security research under the GlitchWitch Security branding to forming an employee owned cybersecurity company. To this day, their infosec journey continues as they build alongside a talented crew of hackers, developers, and wonderful human beings at GlitchSecure.
